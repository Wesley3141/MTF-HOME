Who we are

Marine Turtle Foundation is a student-led group started by Wesley Deklich after seeing hatchlings on beaches in the Yucatan and Florida. We recruit volunteers, run a small number of fundraisers, and send the money to existing turtle centers. We are not a registered 501(c)(3), we do not have a published EIN, and donations to us are not tax-deductible.

Since starting, about 100 people have volunteered with us. We have held four events, including a charity volleyball tournament, and raised about $4,500, which we passed to sea turtle centers in Cancun and Florida. That is the whole operation in one paragraph. Details: Events.

What we do

We recruit volunteers, run cleanups and fundraisers, and point people toward established programs such as Flora, Fauna y Cultura de Mexico on the Riviera Maya.

What we are not

We are not a large NGO, not a Florida 501(c)(3), and not the operator of the national turtle hospitals. Field science is done by the partner programs we support.

Money

About $4,500 raised so far, passed through to Cancun-area and Florida turtle centers. See Donate for how giving actually works.
