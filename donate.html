Donate

Student-led. Not a 501(c)(3). Money goes to turtle centers.

Legal status (read this first)

Marine Turtle Foundation is a student-led initiative founded by Wesley Deklich in Santa Clara County, California. It is not a registered IRS 501(c)(3) public charity. We do not have an EIN listed as a tax-exempt organization. Gifts sent to us are not tax-deductible as charitable contributions to a U.S. 501(c)(3).

We raise money through events and small donations, then pass it to existing sea turtle centers in Cancun, Mexico and Florida. So far we have raised about $4,500 that way. Details are on the Events page.

How to give

We do not collect credit card numbers on this website. If you want to donate, email us and we will send a simple transfer option (such as PayPal or Zelle) and tell you which recipient the next transfer is going to.

  • wesleyd3141@gmail.com
  • marineturtlefoundation@gmail.com
  • 200 West Street, Santa Clara County, California 95070

Who gets the money

Funds are passed to sea turtle programs in Cancun / the Riviera Maya (including work connected with Flora, Fauna y Cultura de Mexico) and to Florida turtle rescue and nesting centers. If you want the exact recipient names for a specific transfer, ask. We will tell you.
